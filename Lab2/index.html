✦ 04 · Help

Questions shopkeepers actually ask.

GST 2.0 slabs at a glance

  • Nil Salt, fresh milk, unbranded atta
  • 5% Packaged food, butter, soap, toothpaste
  • 18% Electricals, most other goods
  • 40% Aerated drinks, tobacco

Illustrative only — confirm the rate for each HSN on the CBIC portal.

When do I charge IGST instead of CGST + SGST?

When the place of supply is a different state from your shop. Karnataka to Karnataka is CGST + SGST; Karnataka to Tamil Nadu is IGST.

Do walk-in customers need a GSTIN?

No. Only registered businesses give a GSTIN, so they can claim input tax credit on what they buy from you.

Why can't I bill an item that is out of stock?

Billing it would push stock below zero and your register would no longer match the shelf. Record the purchase first.
